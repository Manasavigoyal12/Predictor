Documents checklist (TS Medical Counselling)
Keep originals + self-attested copies ready for verification
  • NEET 2026 Admit Card & Rank Card / Scorecard
  • Class 10 Marksheet & Certificate (DOB proof)
  • Class 12 Marksheet & Certificate (PCB marks)
  • Telangana Local/Nativity (Study/Residence) Certificate
  • Caste Certificate — BC-A / BC-B / BC-C / BC-D / SC / ST (Telangana format, competent authority)
  • EWS Income & Asset Certificate (if claiming EWS) — valid for current year
  • Transfer / Migration Certificate from last institution
  • Character Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card & 10+ passport photos (white background)
  • PWD / NCC / Sports certificate — if claiming sub-category
💡 Choice-filling strategy
  • Fill Osmania MC, Gandhi MC, Kakatiya MC Warangal, ESIC Hyderabad first — these close earliest
  • All Telangana government colleges charge the same nominal state tuition — order them by preference only
  • Government seats (~₹40,000/yr) are the most affordable path — always list every reachable one first
  • Private convener (Cat-A) seats are far cheaper than management (Cat-B) or NRI (Cat-C) quota
  • BC-A, BC-B, BC-C and BC-D are four separate reservation blocks in Telangana — confirm which one your certificate covers
  • Never miss the security-deposit / reporting window — missing it forfeits your allotment
  • Understand the Upgrade / Freeze options before Round 2 — a wrong choice can end your journey
