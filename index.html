Karnataka NEET counselling — what to do next
Follow the current KEA and MCC notifications; dates can change.
KEA registration and fee payment
Complete the online application with accurate category and eligibility details.
Document verification
Keep originals, eligibility clauses and category certificates ready.
Option entry and mock allotment
Enter a broad preference list and use mock allotment to improve the order.
KEA Round 1 and Round 2
Review choice/acceptance rules carefully before selecting an option.
Mop-up and stray vacancy rounds
Late-round cutoffs may move sharply when vacancies remain.
MCC counselling for deemed universities
Karnataka deemed colleges are allotted through MCC, not regular KEA state-quota allotment.
Documents checklist
Keep originals and self-attested copies ready
  • NEET 2026 admit card and scorecard
  • Class 10 certificate for date of birth
  • Class 12 marksheet and passing certificate
  • Karnataka study/eligibility documents applicable to your clause
  • KEA application and verification slip
  • Category, caste, income, rural, Kannada or HK/Kalyana Karnataka certificates as applicable
  • Identity proof and recent passport photographs
  • Migration, transfer and gap certificates where applicable
💡 Choice-filling strategy
  • Separate government quota, private quota, minority, NRI and deemed options by budget.
  • Use the exact KEA seat code shown in this report.
  • Do not judge a college only by one round; later rounds can move in either direction.
  • Keep realistic safety choices below your target colleges.
  • Review KEA and MCC rules separately before locking options.
