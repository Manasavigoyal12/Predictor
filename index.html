  • Class 12 Marksheet & Certificate (PCB marks)
  • Haryana Domicile / Bonafide Certificate
  • Caste Certificate — BC-A / BC-B / SC (Haryana format, competent authority)

  • Character Certificate

💡 Choice-filling strategy
  • Fill Pt BD Sharma PGIMS Rohtak, ESIC Faridabad, Kalpana Chawla Karnal first — these close earliest
  • All Haryana government colleges charge the same state tuition — order them by preference only
  • Government seats (~₹80,000–₹1L/yr) are the most affordable path — always list every reachable one first
  • Private state-counselling seats (allotted by DMER Haryana) are cheaper than NRI quota
  • BC-A and BC-B are separate reservation blocks in Haryana — confirm which one you qualify for
  • Never miss the security-deposit / reporting window — missing it forfeits your allotment
  • Understand the Upgrade / Freeze options before Round 2 — a wrong choice can end your journey
