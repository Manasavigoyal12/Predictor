Kerala MBBS counselling - what to do next
Follow CEE Kerala and MCC notifications; exact 2026 dates may change.
Verify NEET details in the KEAM candidate portal
Check score, NEET AIR, state medical rank and category claims.
Review category and document status
Rectify defects within the deadline shown in the CEE portal.
Register and confirm options
List colleges in genuine preference order, not simply cutoff order.
Track Phase 1, Phase 2 and Phase 3 allotments
Cutoffs can move significantly between phases.
Use MCC separately for Amrita Kochi
Deemed-university counselling is not handled through Kerala CEE state allotment.
Documents checklist
Keep originals and readable scanned copies ready.
  • NEET 2026 Admit Card and Scorecard
  • KEAM 2026 application acknowledgement and candidate-portal records
  • Class 10 certificate for date of birth
  • Class 12 marksheet and pass certificate
  • Nativity / state-quota eligibility documents
  • Category, Non-Creamy Layer or EWS certificate, where applicable
  • NRI sponsorship and relationship documents, if claiming NRI quota
  • Transfer, conduct and migration certificates as required
  • Aadhaar / valid identity proof and passport photographs
💡 Choice-filling strategy
  • Place dream colleges first; allotment software follows your option order.
  • Include a balanced mix of government, lower-fee private and safe private options.
  • Do not compare Kerala state-rank cutoffs directly with NEET AIR.
  • Check annual tuition, hostel, special fees and bond conditions before locking.
  • Keep MCC deemed counselling separate from CEE Kerala counselling.
